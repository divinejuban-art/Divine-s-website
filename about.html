The Beginning

Every story starts somewhere.

Hello, I'm Divine! ✨

Welcome to my little corner of the internet. I'm a nursing student who enjoys learning, exploring new ideas, and expressing creativity.

But before nursing, sports, and competitions became part of my story, there were many little experiences that helped shape who I am today.

🥁

My First Big Memory

Grade 1 • A memory I still carry with me

One of my earliest memorable experiences was when I became a logo holder for the Drum and Lyre when I was in Grade 1. 🥁✨

I was still so young, and honestly, I never expected that I would be able to dance during the parade from Sabayle Street to ICNHS at such a young age.

It was such a memorable experience because I got to be part of something bigger than myself while I was still very young.

I can still remember how exciting it was to be part of the parade and to see everyone coming together.

🏆
And the best part?
Our school won! 🎉
🥋

Before Badminton

Learning to compete

Before I became involved in badminton, I also competed in Taekwondo . 🥋

It was one of the experiences that introduced me to competition and taught me what it felt like to prepare, participate, and challenge myself.

Looking back, Taekwondo was one of those early experiences that helped me become familiar with discipline, practice, and the excitement of competing.

I didn't know it at the time, but these early experiences would eventually become part of a much bigger story involving sports, writing, and competitions.

🥋
One experience led to another.
🏸

Discovering Badminton

Another chapter began...

Eventually, badminton became another important part of my school years. 🏸

What started as an interest eventually became something I took more seriously. I had the opportunity to join badminton competitions and experience what it was like to compete and represent my school.

One of the experiences I am especially proud of was being able to participate in the NMRAA twice . 🏸✨

Being able to compete at that level gave me experiences that I will always remember. It taught me about preparation, discipline, perseverance, and the feeling of stepping onto the court knowing that I had worked hard to get there.

Every competition came with its own challenges and memories. Whether it was the excitement before a match, the nervousness during the game, or simply being surrounded by other athletes, each experience became another part of my story.

🏸✨
NMRAA × 2
Two competitions.
Countless memories.
📰

Finding My Voice Through Writing

Sports Writing • RSPC

Sports wasn't the only area where I discovered something new about myself.

I was also able to join the RSPC for Sports Writing , and it became an experience that I will always remember. 📰✨

Being able to participate in an event like that allowed me to experience journalism beyond the usual school setting.

It challenged me to write, observe, and express ideas in a different way. It was an experience that gave me another perspective on competition and allowed me to discover a different side of myself.

📰✨
Another competition.
Another story to tell.
🌙

A Quiet Chapter

My high school years

During high school, not much was happening in terms of competitions.

I eventually stopped joining badminton competitions, and I also stepped away from journalism.

It was a quieter part of my story. Sometimes, life simply takes us through chapters where we pause, change direction, and figure out what we want next.

Even though it felt like I had stepped away from those things, I don't think those experiences ever really left me.

They remained memories that were still part of me, waiting for another chapter.

✨

Finding the Joy Again

Grade 12

Then came Grade 12.

It was during this time that I found the joy of joining competitions again. ✨

After being away from badminton and journalism for a while, getting involved again reminded me of how much I enjoyed challenging myself and being part of something beyond academics.

It felt like an old part of my story was coming back—not exactly the same as before, but with a new perspective.

It reminded me that sometimes the things we temporarily leave behind can still have a place in our lives when the right chapter comes along.

✨
Sometimes a chapter doesn't end.
Sometimes, we simply return to it.
🩺

Where I Am Now

A new chapter

Today, another important chapter of my story is nursing. 🩺💜

I am currently a nursing student, and this journey has taught me that growing into the person I want to become takes patience, courage, and a willingness to keep learning.

When I look back at all the different chapters—from performing in a parade as a young student, competing in Taekwondo and badminton, participating in the NMRAA, writing for competitions, taking a quiet break, and eventually finding that excitement again—I realize that each experience became a small part of who I am today.

These experiences may seem like separate pieces of my life, but together they became part of my story.

And this story isn't finished yet.

“There are still many chapters left to write.” 📖✨
